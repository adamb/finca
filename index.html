About Finca Del Mar

A legacy of luxury and natural beauty

Where Dreams Meet the Ocean

Nestled along Puerto Rico's pristine coastline, Finca Del Mar offers an unparalleled event experience. Our exclusive beachfront property combines natural Caribbean beauty with sophisticated amenities, creating the perfect backdrop for weddings, corporate events, and private celebrations.

Featured in Media

Finca Del Mar gained international recognition as the stunning location for a major Sprite commercial, showcasing our venue's cinematic beauty and prime beachfront setting to audiences worldwide.

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, our versatile spaces adapt to your vision while maintaining the elegance and charm that makes every event extraordinary.
